I wanted opening Sidetrack to feel like a welcoming assistant showing you into
a peaceful, productive place. So it now opens on the Brief: a few sentences
about your day, then just what's yours, due today, this week, or whenever.

Tap anything the greeting mentions to open it. If it says something odd, "What
is this?" shows how it was worked out, and 👎 tells me how to make it better.
