# Changelog posts

A small post about one notable feature or improvement, in Fred's words. Each
shows for 30 days or until it's read or dismissed: as a card at the foot of
the Brief's sidebar, or in a menu in the header on phones.
`/changelog` shows every post on its timeline. When to write one, and how to
draw its picture, is in `AGENTS.md` under "Changelog"; `src/lib/changelog.ts`
checks each file.

```markdown
---
date: 2026-11-21
title: A calmer place to start   # up to 80 characters
image: brief                     # public/posts/brief-{light,dark}.webp
---
A few short paragraphs, blank lines between them, 900 characters at most.

Fred
```
