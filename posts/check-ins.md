My wife wanted someone to keep her going through the day. Not by seeing her
whole board, just what's on today: accountability for the day, not an audit of
her life.

So now you can share your day, or your week, with one person, from the lists
you pick. They see your items there and how many are done on a new Check-ins
tab, plus your mood and day note if you choose. They can comment, and nothing
else on those lists shows.

Share yours from Check-ins, which also shows who sees it, so you can change
what they see or stop whenever you like. They can share theirs back.
